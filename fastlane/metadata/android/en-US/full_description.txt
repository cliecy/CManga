<p>CManga brings local and network comics into one reader, with on-device image enhancement, colorization and text translation.</p>
<h3>Features</h3>
<ul>
<li>Read local comics</li>
<li>Use javascript to create comic sources</li>
<li>Read comics from network sources</li>
<li>Manage favorite comics</li>
<li>Download comics</li>
<li>Enhance comic images with Anime4K and compatible local ONNX models</li>
<li>Colorize black-and-white pages and translate embedded text locally</li>
<li>Sync your library using WebDAV and export your data</li>
<li>View comments, tags, and other information of comics if the source supports</li>
<li>Login to comment, rate, and other operations if the source supports</li>
</ul>
<h3>Thanks</h3>
<p>CManga is an independent app based on open-source reader work, including <a href="https://github.com/venera-app/venera">Venera</a>. It uses its own app identity and storage. Model downloads may have separate licenses and device requirements; network sources require connectivity.</p>
